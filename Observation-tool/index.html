Human Safari Help

What is Human Safari?

Human Safari is an observation tool. You observe people in real life and “catch” them based on what they are doing — just like a game.

How to play:

  1. Look around you (outside, campus, cafe, etc.)
  2. Select a category that matches what you see
  3. Optionally write a short note
  4. Click the Catch! button
  5. Your capture is saved and you earn points

Categories & Points:

  • Phone Zombie — 10 pts
  • Matching Couple — 25 pts
  • Headphones Warrior — 10 pts
  • Food Walker — 15 pts
  • Self Talker — 20 pts
  • Fast Walker — 10 pts
  • Lost Tourist — 30 pts
  • Other — 5 pts

All captures are stored locally and can be downloaded as a CSV dataset for research use.
